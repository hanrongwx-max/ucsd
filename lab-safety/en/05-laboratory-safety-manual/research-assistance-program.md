# Research Assistance Program (RAP)

The EH&S Research Assistance Program combines research safety support and auditing into one team of specialists who help researchers with chemical, radiation, and biological laboratory safety. Contact the RAP specialist for your area.

- **My Research Safety user support:** help using EH&S web applications.
- **Equipment and room clearances:** consults when a lab is closed, renovated, or moved; PIs must leave a vacated lab ready for occupancy or renovation.
- **Hazard reviews & consultations:** lab relocations, custom training, remodel plan reviews, chemical cleanouts.
- **Incident response / review:** investigations and reports. All employees must report injuries; delayed reporting can result in Cal/OSHA fines to the department.
- **Inspections:** RAP audits most research facilities **twice per year**; low-hazard locations annually, under PPM 516. Labs are also audited by other EH&S divisions if they have biosafety containment above BSL-2+, high activity or large quantities of radioactive materials, Class 3b or 4 lasers, or controlled substances. The PI receives an audit report and must address all findings within the required timeframe.
- **New PI EH&S orientation:** contact RAP early (ehsrap@ucsd.edu).
