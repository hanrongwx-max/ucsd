# Be Ready for the Inspector

## EH&S RAP Lab Audits
Twice a year for most research facilities (annually for low-hazard). Prepare with the Lab Self-Audit Form (PDF): https://blink.ucsd.edu/_files/safety-tab/research/lab_self_audit.pdf

## Fire Safety Inspections
The Campus Fire Marshal inspects all occupied campus facilities, including labs.

## Cal/OSHA Inspections
Unannounced visits. Prepare with the Cal/OSHA Inspections Fact Sheet (PDF): https://blink.ucsd.edu/_files/safety-tab/research/UCSD_CAL-OSHA_FactSheet.pdf

## CUPA Inspections
County of San Diego DEH conducts the annual CUPA inspection (usually starting in August), visiting about 4,000 labs, shops, and mechanical spaces.

**CUPA readiness checklist**
- **Biohazardous waste:** double red bags in rigid, leak-proof, covered containers labeled "Biohazard" with the symbol; do not overfill.
- **Chemical waste:** use the OTP; tag each container as soon as waste is added; keep closed and in secondary containment; collected within 90 days. Most common past violation: **ethidium bromide waste** — tag all gel buckets and keep buffer waste containers closed.
- **Empty containers:** determine prior contents and emptiness; follow the disposal flowchart and poster.
- **Chemical storage and labeling:** check compatibility; label hazardous containers (and look-alikes) in English; label disinfection vacuum flasks ("tissue culture media disinfected with bleach 9:1"); post a chemical abbreviation sheet.
- **Compressed gas cylinders:** secure with upper and lower non-combustible restraints; remove regulators and cap unused cylinders.
- **Emergency preparedness:** post the Emergency Guide by the phone; keep the spill kit stocked.
- **Training documentation:** evidence of training for every lab member — "If it's not documented, it didn't happen."
- **Sharps:** do not overfill — anything sticking out is a violation; label by contents.

## DEA Inspections
CS inventory must be available during business hours for unannounced DEA audits. If an inspector arrives without the CSP present, call the CSP immediately.
