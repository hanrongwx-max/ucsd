# Laboratory Safety Manual

The web-based Laboratory Safety Manual is UC San Diego's collection of policies and procedures for campus research and teaching labs. It includes the Chemical Hygiene Plan. Under Cal/OSHA rules, all employees must be informed of the manual's contents, know where it is, and have ready access to it.

## Lab Design

- Any lab that uses hazardous, biohazardous, or radioactive materials, or animals, must have a **hand-washing sink**, connected to building plumbing, near the exit, with paper towel and soap dispensers.
- Handwashing is required after handling potentially hazardous materials and before leaving the lab.

## Laboratory Safety Responsibilities

Lab personnel must:
1. Complete all required health, safety, and environmental training.
2. Review and follow lab authorizations and safety manuals.
3. Follow oral and written lab rules, regulations, and SOPs for assigned tasks.
4. Keep work areas safe and uncluttered.
5. Understand the hazards of materials and processes before starting work.
6. Control identified hazards through engineering controls, PPE, and administrative controls.
7. Understand what their issued PPE can and cannot do.
8. Get PI or supervisor approval before using restricted chemicals or materials.
9. Consult the PI or supervisor before using highly hazardous materials or higher-risk procedures.
10. Report accidents and unsafe conditions immediately to the PI or supervisor.
11. Take part in the medical surveillance program when required.
12. Tell the PI or supervisor about any physician-ordered work restrictions.
13. Follow basic security requirements for hazardous or controlled materials.

The manual explains campus hazard and caution signs; GHS-compliant labels (signal words, pictograms, hazard and precaution statements) appear in chemical inventories.
