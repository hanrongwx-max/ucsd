# Minors in Laboratories

A minor is anyone under 18 (California Family Code §6500-6502). Unaffiliated volunteers must register as visiting scholars or through HR as volunteers before being on site.

## PI Responsibility

PIs must obtain authorization before allowing minors to perform research and may further restrict minors. Each lab with minors must comply with UC CANRA policy; before a minor enters, confirm registration and screening, signed CANRA Mandated Reporter Acknowledgement Forms, completed CANRA training, and review of the Safe Supervision of Minors policy.

## Scope

Covered: any lab or shop using hazardous chemicals, biohazardous/infectious or radioactive materials, radiation-producing equipment, or with physical hazards (compressed gases, high voltage, extreme temperatures, noise, lasers) — summer internships, volunteering, scheduled assignments. Excluded: catalog courses with labs, UC outreach programs, science fair projects.

## Restrictions by Age

| Age | Rule |
|---|---|
| Under 14 | Not allowed in labs, shops, or vivaria except on an approved, supervised tour (including children of lab personnel) |
| Under 18 | Not allowed in vivaria unless IACUC and the vivarium manager approve |
| Under 18 | **Never** permitted where controlled-substance research is performed |
| 14–18 | Allowed if enrolled in a catalog course / approved supervised tour, **or** with written parent/guardian consent, required safety training and PPE, and accompanied by a trained supervisor **at all times** |
