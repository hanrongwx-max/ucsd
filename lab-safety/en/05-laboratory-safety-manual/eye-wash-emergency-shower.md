# Eye Wash and Emergency Shower Guidelines

Applies to all UC San Diego laboratories, shops, and studios. If you work with hazardous chemicals, know where the nearest eye wash and emergency shower are and how to use them.

## Location and Accessibility
- Reachable **within 10 seconds** along an unobstructed path (ANSI: about 55 feet).
- One person must be able to use both eyewash and shower at once if both are needed.
- Ideally no doors; at most one door swinging in the direction of travel.

## Approved Equipment Only
- Sink-mounted eye washes count only with two orifices. Deluge hoses are discouraged. Never rely on sink faucets.

## Operation and Maintenance
- Most units are hands-free (paddle, lever, pull-lever); reverse the action to turn off.
- Keep the area clear; keep energized electrical equipment away.
- Keep spout caps on; check the inspection tag — **EH&S inspects monthly**. Report problems: (858) 534-3660.
- Never use safety equipment for cleaning or non-emergency tasks.

## Using the Eye Wash
The first few seconds are critical, especially with corrosives.
1. Flush eyes immediately for **at least 15 minutes**.
2. Hold eyes open and rotate the eyeballs in all directions.
3. Call Poison Control **(800) 222-1222**, then seek medical attention.
4. Have someone bring the SDS to the doctor.
5. Report the injury or exposure.

## Using the Emergency Shower
1. Flush with large amounts of water for **at least 15 minutes**, protecting the eyes.
2. Remove contaminated clothing, jewelry, and shoes without delay (use a clean lab coat for privacy and warmth).
3. Call Poison Control (800) 222-1222 and seek medical attention.
4. Bring the SDS to the doctor.
5. Report the injury or exposure.

Rusty water at first or no floor drain is not a reason to delay — call Facilities Management (858) 534-2930 to collect water afterwards.

Regulations: CCR Title 8 §5162; California Plumbing Code §416.0
