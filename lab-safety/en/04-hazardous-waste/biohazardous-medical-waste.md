# Biohazardous and Medical Waste

## Key Timing Requirement

Lab-generated biohazardous waste "must be at the EH&S Environmental Management Facility (EMF) within **7 calendar days**" of the first waste being placed in the biohazard bag (County of San Diego CUPA). Labs must set a routine weekly schedule for moving waste to their building's accumulation area.

Biohazardous waste includes medical, infectious, sharps, and clinical medical waste contaminated with infectious agents, recombinant or synthetic nucleic acids, human blood, body fluids, or OPIM.

## 1. Animal Carcasses and Animal Waste
- Includes all genetically modified animals (e.g., all mice and zebrafish).
- Clear colorless bag → red pathological waste container (tight lid, red bag liner) → refrigerator or freezer; max **7 days**.
- Label: biohazard symbol, IACUC protocol number, date, species, PI name, campus address.
- Off-site incineration. Animal Care Program: (858) 534-6064.

## 2. Red Bag Waste
- Examples: plastic pipettes, tips, Petri dishes, culture flasks, paper towels used on contaminated surfaces, bench paper, biohazardous animal bedding, contaminated gloves, GM organisms.
- **Free-flowing liquids in biohazard waste are not compliant**; tubes with minimal liquid are OK with absorbent in the bag.
- Two red biohazard bags in a rigid, leakproof container with tight lid; label biohazard symbol on lid and sides plus building, room, phone.
- When full or before 7 days: tie the neck in a single knot → transport in a biohazard secondary container → building accumulation site → red transport bins.
- Move waste at least once per week; accumulation sites are **not serviced on weekends or holidays**.

## 3. Pathological Waste
- Human anatomical specimens from Anatomical Services must be returned to them: (858) 534-4126; preparation (858) 534-4546 / 534-8280.
- Other human tissues and animal parts: red biohazard bag → red pathological container → refrigerate/freeze; call EMF (858) 534-2753.

## 4. Liquid Biohazardous Waste
- Vacuum flask with stopper; secondary container outside a BSC; max 7 days; label biohazard symbol and "media waste and bleach (9:1)".
- Treat with bleach **1:9 for 30 minutes** then pour down the lab sink with PPE, or autoclave without bleach in a permitted autoclave.
- Bleach is the only university-wide approved chemical disinfectant.

## 5. Sharps
- Needles, syringes, blades, Pasteur pipettes, blood vials, contaminated glassware.
- Biohazard-contaminated sharps → FDA-approved rigid, leakproof, puncture-resistant sharps container. **No chemical- or radioactive-contaminated sharps** in biohazard sharps containers; no gloves, Kimwipes, or debris.
- Close and tape the container; place it in a red biohazardous waste collection bin.

## 6. Other Waste Types
- Radioactive; plastic tissue-culture pipettes; chemically contaminated (hazwaste@ucsd.edu); mixed waste (EMF (858) 534-2753); recognizable body parts (Anatomical Services (858) 534-8280).
