# Wash and Rinse Water Disposal

How to dispose of wash and rinse water contaminated with hazardous materials or with highly toxic, acidic, or caustic cleaning solutions, when washing glassware, containers, and equipment.

## 1. Can It Go Down the Drain?

Only if it is nonhazardous (no radioactive, untreated biohazardous, or hazardous chemical waste), liquid, and nondisruptive (no corrosive pH, grease/oil, or hot temperatures ≥150° in volumes over 10 gallons). Otherwise go to Step 2.

## 2. Collect Contaminated Rinse Water

- Empty equipment and glassware that held hazardous materials into an appropriate hazardous waste container.
- Wash with a small amount of water or appropriate solvent; empty the rinse into the waste container.
- Rinse and empty once more.

**Washing equipment:** detergents, wash and rinse water can go down the drain *unless* highly caustic, acidic, or toxic cleaning solutions were used (Chromerge, sodium hydroxide, sulfuric acid, hydrochloric acid, phosphoric acid wash solutions). Avoid these if possible. Collect such wash water as hazardous waste; capture the first rinse (reuse it in the wash solution or put it in the waste container). Additional rinses can go down the drain.

## 3. Dispose of the Hazardous Waste

Label with a hazardous waste tag and request a hazardous waste collection.
