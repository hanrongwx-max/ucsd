# Chemcycle

Chemcycle is UC San Diego's chemical recycling program, operated by EH&S. Researchers can get free chemicals and donate usable, qualifying surplus chemicals. Chemicals obtained are "solely for research use on campus."

Alert (at time of retrieval): Chemcycle is undergoing maintenance ahead of a major upgrade launching in 2026; the app is temporarily unavailable.

## How to Use

1. Search the Chemcycle inventory (https://ehs.ucsd.edu/chemcycle). 2. Select chemicals. 3. Submit the request form. 4. Enter contact details.

Delivery: standard — next scheduled hazardous waste collection for your building; urgent — call the EMF at (858) 534-2753.

## Donate Chemicals

**Accepted:** non-potential-peroxide-forming, virgin, unused, factory-sealed containers in the original unopened manufacturer container with labels intact (e.g., ethanol, glycerol, 2-mercaptoethanol).

**Not accepted:** mixtures, solutions, samples; expired or degraded chemicals; research by-products, spent material, hazardous waste; DEA controlled substances; DEA List I and II chemicals; California precursors and select agents; radioactive materials.

**How to donate:** create a tag in the OTP selecting "Chemcycle" as content type → print the tag and request pickup → label the container and place it in your hazardous waste collection area → technicians collect it at the next scheduled collection.

Contact: chemcycle@ucsd.edu, (858) 534-2753
