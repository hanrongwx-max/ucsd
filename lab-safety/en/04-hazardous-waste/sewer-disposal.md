# Sewer Disposal: What Can Go Down the Drain?

"Whatever goes down the drain ends up in the ocean." The San Diego Metropolitan Wastewater Department strictly regulates sewer waste.

## What Can Go Down the Drain

A substance is allowed down drains only if it meets **all** of these criteria:

1. **Nonhazardous** — no radioactive waste; no hazardous chemical waste; no untreated biohazardous waste.
2. **Liquid** — no solids, sludges, or viscous substances.
3. **Won't interfere with sewage treatment** — no corrosive pH; no grease or oil; no hot liquids (150 °F or higher) in volumes over 10 gallons.

Unsure? Contact the EH&S Environmental Management Facility, hazwaste@ucsd.edu or (858) 534-3660.

Protect drains from chemical spills. Don't use sinks for chemical storage or secondary containment. "Disposal of hazardous waste using sinks, intentional evaporation, or as regular trash is against the law."

## By Material

| Material | Down the drain? |
|---|---|
| Biohazardous solids | Not allowed |
| Biohazardous liquids | Only after deactivation: bleach 1 part to 9 parts liquid waste for 30 minutes, or autoclave (without bleach) |
| Controlled substances | Not allowed |
| Radioactive waste | Not allowed |
| Hazardous chemical waste | Not allowed |
| Solids, sludges, viscous substances | Not allowed |
| Powders and salts | Not allowed — **do not dissolve them in water** |
| Corrosive waste, pH 2.0–5.0 | Only if adjusted to above 5.0 and below 12.5; otherwise hazardous waste |
| Corrosive waste, pH ≤ 2.0 or ≥ 12.5 | Never — always hazardous chemical waste; **do not adjust** |
| Hot nonhazardous liquids ≥ 150 °F | Not in volumes of 10 gallons or more (cool first) |
| Grease and oil | Not at ≥ 500 mg/L. Automotive/gear oil and machinery grease → hazardous waste; vegetable oil, lard, shortening → regular trash |
| Alcohols | Only at nonhazardous concentration; ethanol is nonhazardous below 24%. **Diluting is not allowed** |
| Ethidium bromide | Not allowed at any concentration (hazardous waste) |
| Formalin / formaldehyde | Allowed if < 2.9% formaldehyde and no methanol or other hazardous chemicals; diluting not allowed |
| Hydrogen peroxide | Only if < 8%; diluting not allowed |
| Photo / X-ray chemicals | Only spent developer not mixed with fixer |
| Other chemicals | Only at nonhazardous concentration; diluting not allowed |
| Latex paint, oil paint | Not allowed |
| Wash and rinse water | Not if contaminated by hazardous materials or highly caustic, acidic, or toxic cleaning solutions |

Bleach is the only university-wide approved chemical disinfectant. To request an alternative, email EHSBIO@UCSD.EDU with material, chemical, concentration, contact time, and disposal method (response within five working days).
