# Waste Generator Number (WGN) Overview

- Chemical and radioactive hazardous waste at UC San Diego is tracked by a WGN.
- Generators **must apply for and receive a WGN** before EH&S can collect their waste.
- Individual generators are not charged for waste disposal.
- Application information is used to track waste and contact generators about contents, labeling, or containers.

## Applying

- Apply online: https://blink.ucsd.edu/safety/ehs/forms-ehs/waste-generator.html
- Multiple facilities within the same department share one WGN; generators with multiple departmental assignments apply separately for each.
- Receiving a WGN automatically creates an OTP account.
