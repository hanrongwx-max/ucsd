# How to Request a Hazardous Waste Collection

The EH&S Environmental Management Facility collects, processes, and disposes of chemical, radioactive, chemical-radioactive, and universal hazardous waste.

- Hazardous Waste Collection Schedule (PDF) — see link on the source page.
- Biohazardous and medical waste: see the packaging and disposal page. Non-hazardous waste: see Non-Hazardous Waste Management.

## Special Pickups

Available for large volumes, numerous items, or large items (e.g., refrigerators). For a lab clean-out or project producing large volumes, contact your RAP Specialist. Equipment that may have contacted radioactive, biohazardous, or chemical materials must be **decontaminated before collection** (request a decontamination clearance if required).

## How to Request

Use the Online Waste Tag Program (OTP) to create and print tags, request pickup, and view history. **Pick-up requests must be submitted no later than the day before your building's scheduled collection.** Requests submitted on the collection day roll to the next cycle.
