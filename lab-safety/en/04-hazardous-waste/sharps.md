# How to Manage Sharps

Sharps are devices or objects with corners, edges, or projections that can cut or pierce skin or ordinary waste bags: hypodermic needles, syringes, tubing; blunted needles; blades (scalpels, razors, microtomes).

## General Guidelines

- **Do not recap needles.**
- Keep sharps pointed away from yourself and others; never leave exposed sharps unattended.
- Dispose of sharps immediately after use.
- Store safely: secure with a magnet; razor blades in a Petri dish; needles in a cork; avoid placing sharps on the bench.
- Do not bend, shear, or tamper with sharps.
- Transport in a lidded, rigid, puncture-proof, labeled container.
- Keep a sharps container near where sharps are generated; fill only to the **"fill" line**.
- Keep non-sharps (gloves, Kimwipes, wrappers) out of biohazard sharps containers.

## Five Disposal Categories (separation required by law)

1. **Non-contaminated** — rigid, leak-proof, puncture-resistant container; deface biohazard symbols; OTP label "non-hazardous sharps"; no free liquids; tape shut; request pickup via OTP. Custodial staff will not collect broken-glass boxes over 50 lb.
2. **Biohazardous** — container marked "Biohazard"; label with biohazard symbol and UC San Diego address, building and room; no chemicals/radioactives; move full containers to the biohazard collection site **within 7 days** of closing.
3. **Chemically contaminated** — hazardous waste tag, Chemical Waste option, constituent "Chemical Sharps"; request EH&S collection.
4. **Radioactive** — handle first as radioactive waste; tag lists isotope, other hazards, deactivation method, type "other – SHARPS". **Do not bleach iodine isotopes.** Store in the radioactive waste area.
5. **Laboratory glassware** — see Empty Research Container Management.

## Improper Disposal Found

- In a waste receptacle: remove from use; put on safety glasses, lab coat, gloves; place the container/bag inside a sharps container; dispose; remove PPE and wash hands.
- In an open area: secure the area; PPE; choose the matching sharps container; collect with tongs or broom and dustpan; never by hand.
- Unknown hazard: cordon off and contact ehsrap@ucsd.edu. Report unknown-source incidents as a near miss.

## Training

Complete training before using sharp devices, including lab-specific training from senior personnel.
