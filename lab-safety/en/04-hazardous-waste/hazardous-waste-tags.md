# How to Use UC San Diego Hazardous Waste Tags

The Online Waste Tag Program (OTP) is the preferred way to request hazardous waste pickup. **Do not create duplicate waste tags** — duplicates can cause missed collections.

## Step 1: Get a Waste Generator Number (WGN)

Hazardous waste generators need a WGN to use the OTP, and EH&S cannot collect waste until a WGN is assigned. Once assigned, you automatically get an OTP account.

## When to Use a Hazardous Waste Tag

- Containers must be labeled for storage, transportation, and disposal under the law.
- The tag must show **ownership, accumulation start date, contents, and hazards**, and must be **securely attached before any waste is added**.
- Use tags for chemical, radioactive, mixed chemical-radioactive, and universal waste.
- Biohazardous waste with no chemical or radioactive contamination is labeled directly on the red biosafety bag or sharps container — not with a hazardous waste tag.
- Label promptly with complete information to avoid violations and penalties.

## Get Waste Tags Through the OTP

You need your lab's WGN and a Business Systems (SSO) login. The OTP tracks wastes, generates tags for almost all hazardous waste, saves frequent profiles, fills in hazard class automatically, notifies EH&S for pickup, and keeps a history. Questions: hazwaste@ucsd.edu, (858) 534-9984 or (858) 534-2753.

## Request Waste Pickup

All hazardous waste must be collected within **90 days** of when it was first placed in containers.

## Waste Refusal Notice

If tag information is missing, technicians may refuse the waste and leave a notice. Complete the missing information before the next scheduled collection; after a second refusal, a technician will contact you.

## Recurring Container Use Accumulation Tags

Accumulation containers reused for the same waste and emptied into a larger container need a recurring accumulation use tag: specify days of use (daily containers emptied at least once a day while in use), location, name, building, phone, chemical name, hazard class and form. Attach a complete Online Waste Tag to the larger container before the recurring container is used. Tag (PDF): https://blink.ucsd.edu/_files/safety-tab/research/Recurring_Container_Use_Accumulation_Tag.pdf

## Order Waste Tag Holders

In the OTP, select the option to order more holders (sent by campus mail).

Contact: hazwaste@ucsd.edu, (858) 534-2753. Short link: http://blink.ucsd.edu/go/wastetag
