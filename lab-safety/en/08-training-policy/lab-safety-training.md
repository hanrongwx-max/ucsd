# Laboratory Safety Training

Determine your Research Facility Classification first. PIs and supervisors must identify job-specific hazards and ensure training. **Training records must be kept for at least 3 years.**

## Principal Investigators
- **New PI Orientation** — required before operating a lab or supervising anyone; schedule via ehsrap@ucsd.edu.
- Supervisor Safety eCourse (40 min, optional).

## All Laboratory Workers

| Training | When | Refresher |
|---|---|---|
| New Laboratory Worker Checklist (PDF) | Before work | — |
| UC Laboratory Safety Fundamentals | Before beginning work | Every 3 years (Laboratory Safety Refresher) |
| Annual Laboratory Hazards Training | Before beginning work | Annually (every 3 years if not in your own research lab) |
| Laboratory Hazard Assessment (LHAT) and Lab-Specific PPE Training | Each new lab member | When LHAT changes |
| Laboratory Ergonomics | Recommended for repetitive tasks | — |

UC Laboratory Safety Fundamentals also meets Hazard Communication (GHS label/SDS) training.

## Area Safety Coordinators
ASC Orientation recommended (ehsrap@ucsd.edu, (858) 822-6886).

## Non-UC San Diego Students and Staff
1. Register for an affiliate account through your DSA (up to 48 hours).
2. Ask your Lab Safety Contact to add you to the Lab Roster, LHAT, and, if applicable, HCPs, BUA, LUA, RUA (username usually starts with "AFF").
Then complete the same required training.

## Technical Research Areas
May require Annual Shop Studio Environmental Compliance Hazards Training.

Videos supplement but do not replace training. Opt-out of refresher assignments via the email link if you no longer need the training (2–3 business days).
