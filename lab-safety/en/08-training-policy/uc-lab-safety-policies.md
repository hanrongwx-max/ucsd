# UC Laboratory Safety Policies

Three systemwide UC policies set "minimum requirements for all UC laboratory workers": PPE, laboratory safety training, and minors in labs and shops. Campuses may exceed them.

## PPE
- **Minimum attire at all times:** full-length pants (or equivalent) and closed-toe, closed-heel shoes.
- **LHAT** required for research spaces with hazardous materials or equipment; it summarizes hazards and specifies PPE.
- Additional PPE (flame-resistant lab coat, face shield, splash goggles) per hazard assessment.
- Personnel in adjacent areas who could be exposed must wear a lab coat and safety glasses.
- Centrally funded PPE Distribution Program.
- "Worker" = someone actively performing work with hazardous materials or equipment (faculty, staff, student volunteers, visitors, visiting scholars); not passive tour/lecture attendees.

## Lab Safety Training

| Course | Refresher |
|---|---|
| UC Laboratory Safety Fundamentals | Every 3 years |
| Annual Laboratory Hazards Training | Annually |

## Minors in Labs and Shops
- Under 14: not permitted except approved, supervised tours (includes children of lab personnel).
- Unaffiliated volunteers must register and be vaccinated before on-site activity.
- Vivaria: only with IACUC and facility manager approval.
- 14–18: written parent/guardian consent, direct supervision by a qualified adult at all times, training and PPE.
