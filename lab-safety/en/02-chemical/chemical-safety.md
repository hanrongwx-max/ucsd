# Chemical Safety

This page gathers the safety requirements, training, and standard operating procedures (SOPs) for working with chemicals at UC San Diego.

## Hazard Control Plans (HCPs)
- Hazardous chemical work requires an HCP, which must be obtained and followed.
- At UC San Diego, an HCP serves as the SOP.
- Resources: the My Research Safety portal and guidance on how to use HCPs.

## Chemical Hygiene Plan (CHP)
- The online CHP must be readily accessible to personnel in any facility that uses hazardous chemicals.
- A Cal/OSHA inspection fact sheet explains how to prepare for inspections: "Be Ready for the Inspector!" (PDF) — https://blink.ucsd.edu/_files/safety-tab/research/UCSD_CAL-OSHA_FactSheet.pdf

## Chemical Safety Guidelines
- General chemical safety guidelines
- Specific guidelines (SOPs for particularly high-hazard chemicals)
- Globally Harmonized System (GHS) of classification and labeling
- California Green Chemistry Initiative
- Chemical safety information from the UC San Diego Library

## Chemical Safety & Surveillance Committee (CSSC)
Responsible for: advising the Chancellor on the safe use of hazardous chemicals; reducing risks from hazardous chemicals; creating and enforcing chemical safety policies and procedures; disciplining willful or negligent violations of chemical safety practices.

- Storage and inventory guidelines; Chemical Inventory brochure (PDF); chemical abbreviations and annotations (PDF/Word); chemical compatibility guidelines; compressed gas storage; GHS; flammable and combustible liquids storage

- Forms; PI research safety authorizations and new-PI resources; Hazard Communication Program for non-lab facilities; Area Safety Coordinators; preventing reproductive hazards; ChemCycle (chemical reuse) and the Chemistry & Biochemistry Stockroom
