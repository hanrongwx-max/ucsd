# Specific Chemical Guidelines

Requirements and procedures for work with particularly hazardous chemicals at UC San Diego.

## Requirement for Researchers

Researchers must use the Hazard Control Plan (HCP) application — the Chemical Hazard Use Application (CHUA) — to create hazard control plans for materials on the high-hazard chemicals list. "At UC San Diego, a hazard control plan is a standard operating procedure (SOP)."

### High-Hazard Chemicals List

1. Anesthetic Gases
2. Antineoplastic or Investigational New Drugs
3. Chemical Carcinogens
4. Compressed Gas
5. Cryogenic Liquids
6. Ethidium Bromide
7. Flammable and Combustible Liquids
8. Nanotechnology
9. Organic Peroxide Formers
10. Perchloric Acid
11. Picric Acid
12. Photographic Processing Hazards
13. Piranha Solution
14. Pyrophoric and Other Highly Reactive Materials
15. Sodium Azide
16. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Library, UC Riverside (external): http://ehs.ucr.edu/laboratory/SOP/index.html

## Notice: Hazardous Waste Disposal

Disposing of hazardous waste by pouring it down sinks, letting it evaporate intentionally, or putting it in regular trash is prohibited. Campus laboratories must follow strict state and federal waste disposal requirements; individuals may be held liable for violations.
