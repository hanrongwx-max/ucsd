# Controlled Substances Program (CSP)

## Overview

The CSP governs the use of controlled substances (CS) — drugs or chemicals whose manufacture, possession, and use the government regulates. The campus holds federal DEA registrations for CS use in research, veterinary care, and teaching. EH&S administers the program through the CS Program Manager.

## Is a chemical a CS?

At UC San Diego, CS include all DEA-scheduled substances, DEA List 1 chemicals, and California Precursor Chemicals. An approved **Controlled Substance Use Authorization (CSUA)** is required before work begins.

## CSUA

- PIs must obtain a CSUA to possess and use CS for research, teaching, or veterinary care. (Clinical trials through the pharmacy/Investigational Drug Service do not require one.)
- The online CSUA system lets users apply, designate lab contacts, manage authorized personnel, upload PSDS forms, monitor training, order CS, manage inventory, request disposal, and amend/renew/deactivate.

## Roles and Responsibilities

**PIs**
- Keep a current CSUA, an accurate online inventory, and an on-site CS Log Book at all times; supervise the CS facility and Authorized Personnel.
- Maintain secure, restricted-access storage in a CSP-approved area; keep CS Usage Logs and Log Books there.
- Complete the Controlled Substances Training eCourse (UC Learning Center); **refresher every 4 years**.
- Complete a PSDS-PI form.
- During sabbatical, an acting PI must be named in writing and approved.

**Authorized Personnel (AP)**
- Use CS only for the purpose stated in the CSUA.
- Keep CS in the approved secure storage at all times; return it when not in use.
- Record every dispensation on the CS Usage Log.
- Do not share CS with unauthorized personnel or other labs; do not dispense or log out CS for others.
- Dispose of CS properly.
- Report inventory discrepancies, suspected misuse, or theft to the CS Program Manager immediately.
- Complete CS training (refresher every 4 years) and a PSDS-Authorized Personnel form.

**Department Chairs** — notify the CSP of arriving/departing PIs with CS; approve or reject CSUA applications (not their own); ensure inventories are maintained.

## Personnel Screening Data Sheet (PSDS)

Applicants answer DEA-required questions on CS-related felony convictions; surrendered/revoked/denied registrations; and non-prescribed use of narcotics, amphetamines, or barbiturates.

## Storage, Ordering, Inventory, Disposal

See Storage Requirements, How to Order, Managing Inventory, and CS Disposal pages.

## Inspections

- EH&S, IACUC, and the DEA may inspect CS inventory. **The DEA conducts unannounced inspections.**
- Researchers must conduct regular self-audits (Lab Self-Audit Form and CS Self-Assessment form).
- IACUC checks for expired CS used in animals — label "Expired Not for Use in Animals" and request disposal.

## Reporting Problems

Report any CS problems (log/disposal discrepancies, suspected theft or loss) **immediately** to the PI and the CSP. The CSP contacts UCPD and the DEA when required. Illicit activity can be reported anonymously.
