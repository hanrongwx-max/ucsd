# Radiation Safety

UC San Diego's institutional radiation safety program (clinical and research).

- **EH&S Services Lab:** Campus Services Complex, Bldg E 119 (Greenhouse Lane), (858) 534-6418, Mail Code 0035.
- **Exposure monitoring:** overview; dosimetry services; radiation badge and ring user guidelines; pregnancy and fetal monitoring.
- **Laser safety:** program; Laser Use Authorization (LUA); forms; training.
- **Magnet safety:** guidelines; continuous exposure limits.
- **Purchasing radioactive materials:** Radiation Safety must survey and inventory all incoming radioactive materials. Ship to the Campus Services Complex address and include your RUA — "Failure to use the shipping address and not including your RUA will result in Marketplace delays and processing delays." Outside procurement: UC San Diego Radiation Safety, Campus Services Complex Bldg E 119, La Jolla, CA 92093-0035, Attn: PI Name, RUA #.
- **Survey meters:** calibration/repair; loan program; decontamination clearance.
- **Radiation Safety Manual:** UC San Diego uses radioactive materials under a Broad Scope Radioactive Material License from the State of California covering campus, Medical Center, and Scripps Institution of Oceanography. All PIs and users must follow the manual and their **Radioisotope Use Authorization (RUA)**.
- **Committees:** Radiation Safety Committee; Human Exposure Review Committee; Radioactive Drug Research Committee; Medical Center Radiation Safety Committee.

Contact: EH&S Radiation Safety, ehsrad@ucsd.edu, (858) 822-2494
