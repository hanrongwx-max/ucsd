## Physical Hazards
- Wet floors or spills and clutter can lead to lab slips, trips, and falls.
- Requirements under OSHA's Walking-Working Surfaces Standard (29 CFR 1910.22):
  - Keep floors clean and dry (29 CFR 1910.22(a)(2)).
  - Keep aisles and passageways clear and in good repair, with no obstruction that could create a hazard (29 CFR 1910.22(a)).
- UC Lesson Learned: Water from Freezer Ice Causes Slip and Fall — https://cls.ucla.edu/water-freezer-ice-causes-slip-and-fall
