# Report Safety Concerns and Near Misses

Report safety concerns and near misses to EH&S. (Employee injuries use the separate injury-reporting guide.)

- **Non-emergency reports** (health or safety concerns, environmental issues, non-employee injuries): online Safety Concern Form, or **anonymously** by calling EH&S (858) 534-3660.
- **Near misses:** conditions or events with the potential for serious consequences.
- **Feedback:** what EH&S does well or could improve.

## Emergency
For imminent hazards, threats, or emergencies: UC San Diego Police — 9-1-1 from campus phones, (858) 534-4357 from other phones; your supervisor or department head; EH&S (858) 534-3660 (weekdays 8:00–4:30).

Contact: ehsweb@ucsd.edu — Shortcut: blink.ucsd.edu/go/safetyconcern
