## Fire
Report all fires and explosions to police by calling 911, even if out.
- **Small fire:** pull the alarm and call 911; alert others; use an extinguisher only with an exit behind you, within six feet, using **PASS** (Pull, Aim at the base, Squeeze, Sweep); evacuate by stairs; account for coworkers; wait at the assembly area.
- **Large fire:** alarm and 911; alert others; close doors and windows if safe; evacuate; assist responders.
- Report all fires to the Campus Fire Marshal (Fire Incident Form).

## Utility Failure
Contact Facilities. Smoke/burning odor: evacuate. Flooding: disconnect electrical devices if safe, evacuate. **Gas leak:** evacuate immediately, call 911 from a safe area, no spark-producing devices.

## Earthquake
**Drop, cover, and hold on**; otherwise go to an interior wall away from heavy equipment and protect your head; avoid windows and shelving. Afterwards: check for injured people and ruptured utilities; evacuate by stairs; go to the assembly area.

## Tsunami
Advisory / Watch / Warning levels; after a coastal earthquake move inland to higher ground.

## Biological Spill
EH&S Spill Response (858) 534-3660; after hours 911.
1. Clear the area; help injured or contaminated people.
2. Rinse splashes at an eyewash/shower for at least 15 minutes; seek medical attention.
3. Remove contaminated clothing into a biohazard bag.
4. **Wait at least 30 minutes** for aerosols to settle.
5. Wear lab coat, safety glasses, gloves.
6. Cover with dry paper towels, then disinfectant-soaked towels; surround with disinfectant.
7. Allow **20 minutes** of contact time.
8. Dispose as biohazardous waste; disinfect a second time.

## Needlestick or Exposure to Blood or Body Fluids
- Wash the wound with soap and water; rinse mucous membranes at an eyewash.
- Report immediately to EH&S (858) 534-3660 and submit an Employee Incident Report.
- Business hours: page 1447 via (619) 543-6737 or call COEM. After hours: emergency room (Thornton or UC San Diego Medical Center).
- Post-exposure prophylaxis works best when started early — seek advice even if hours or days have passed.

## Chemical or Radiation Spill
**Minor (lab staff can clean up):** alert people and the PI; avoid vapors; identify the substance; rinse skin/eyes 15 minutes and call Poison Control; for radiation, survey personnel, decontaminate skin, record cpm and time, notify EH&S; confine the spill (radioactive tape, isotope noted); PPE (goggles, disposable gloves, shoe covers, long-sleeve coat); clean from the **outside edges toward the center**; double clear bags with a waste tag; clean with water; for radiation, monitor until clear and give EH&S a written record.

**Major:** attend to injured; rinse 15 minutes; avoid vapors; turn off ignition sources if safe; evacuate, close doors, prevent entry; call EH&S Spill Response immediately.

## Bomb Threat
Do not search or touch suspicious objects; report to police; for phone threats stay calm, keep the caller talking, and note details.

## Acts of Violence / Active Shooter
Call or text 911 when safe; silence your phone; **Run, Hide, Fight** (fight only as a last resort).

## Personal Injury
- First aid if trained; 911 for emergencies; Occupational Health clinics weekdays 8–4:30; emergency departments after hours.
- Hazardous material involved: Poison Control (800) 222-1222.
- Report to Workers' Compensation and submit an Employee Incident Report.
- Serious incidents: call 911, secure the scene, preserve evidence for at least 24 hours.
- **Clothing on fire:** drop and roll, or use the emergency shower.
- **Hazardous material in eyes or on skin:** rinse 15 minutes.
- **Minor cuts:** wash several minutes; a tetanus booster may be needed.

## Building Evacuation
Take personal items; close doors without locking; nearest EXIT; help others; go to the assembly area and take a headcount; tell responders about sensitive research, running equipment, and animals; do not re-enter until authorized.
