# Environment, Health & Safety (EH&S)

UC San Diego Environment, Health & Safety (EH&S) manages risk and provides health, safety, and environmental services to the campus and the local community. The page organizes its resources into safety areas, reporting channels (workplace injuries, safety concerns), forms, public health information, workplace violence guidance, recharge rates, awards, and training.
