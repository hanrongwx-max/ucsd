# How to Use a Centrifuge Safely

Main hazards: **mechanical failure** and **aerosol creation**. Researchers must complete a PI-preapproved "Centrifuge" HCP.

## Mechanical Failure
- Follow manufacturer instructions; inspect often; train users; post operating instructions.
- Rotors fatigue — discard after the use limit; use the correct rotor for the centrifuge; observe maximum speed and density ratings; match ultracentrifuge classification decals.
- Keep a **logbook for each ultracentrifuge rotor** (duration and speed of every run) at the instrument; if lost, replace the rotors and start a new log.

## Aerosols
Generated when filling, uncapping, removing supernatant, resuspending, and centrifuging; the greatest hazard is a tube breaking during a run.
- Fill and open tubes/rotors in a BSC; use sealed tubes and O-ring safety buckets; inspect before each use.
- **Never cap tubes with aluminum foil.** Avoid cellulose nitrate tubes.
- Don't overfill; wipe sealed tubes with disinfectant; balance properly.
- Don't decant supernatant — use a vacuum system with in-line traps and filters.
- Resuspend by swirling; if shaking, wait before opening.

## Before Running
Ventilated designated space; clean, dry rotor properly seated; **balance tubes** (½ g at 1 G ≈ 250 kg at 500,000 G); reduce speed for dense solutions; confirm run speed every time.

## If a Tube Breaks
Turn off; **wait 15 minutes** before opening; call EH&S (858) 534-3660 if unsure; disinfect the rotor (in a hood if possible).

## After the Run
Confirm the rotor has stopped before opening; clean and disinfect; never leave a centrifuge contaminated; never immerse swinging-bucket rotor bodies.

## Infectious Materials
HEPA filter between chamber and vacuum pump when containment is needed; load/unload in a BSC.

## PPE
Lab coat (sleeves to wrist); safety glasses or splash goggles plus face shield; compatible gloves; full-length pants; closed-toe shoes.

## Biohazardous Spill in a Centrifuge
Clear the area; **wait 30 minutes**; lab coat, glasses, gloves; move rotors/buckets to a BSC; disinfect the interior; dispose as biohazardous waste.

## Exposure
Skin/eyes: flush 15 minutes; ingestion: immediate medical attention; ambulance via Campus Police (858) 534-4357; Poison Control (800) 222-1222.

## Storage
Not in corridors or offices; fixed-angle rotors upside down with lids removed; rotors dry and outside the centrifuge; remove expired components.
