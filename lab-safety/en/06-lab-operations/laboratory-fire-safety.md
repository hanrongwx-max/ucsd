# Laboratory Fire Safety Inspection

The campus fire marshal works with RAP to inspect all campus labs against CCR Title 19, the California Fire Code, and campus policy (no fire drill involved).

| Area | Inspectors look for / Requirements |
|---|---|
| Biosafety cabinets | Open flames and flammable gases prohibited in BSCs unless approved by EH&S and the Department Chair; gas lines removed; new BSCs not plumbed for gas |
| CFC chemical allowance | Keep inventory within fire code MAQs by control area (DSOs monitor via My Research Safety) |
| Chemical storage | Segregate and label incompatibles; pyrophorics only in fully sprinklered buildings |
| Compressed gases | Approved HCP; cylinders secured at all times |
| Ceilings and walls | Keep intact; any penetration must be approved by Facilities and Fire & Life Safety |
| Corridors | No obstructions; exit signs and lighting; fire doors not propped open |
| Electrical | No frayed cords, overloaded outlets, daisy-chained adaptors, extension cords, uncovered boxes, blocked/unlabeled panels |
| Exits | "Keep exit doors closed, unobstructed, and operational"; exit signs visible and lit |
| Extinguishers | ABC extinguisher within **75 feet** of travel and at least one in the lab; min rating **4A:60B:C**; specialty (CO2, Class D) in addition; inspect **monthly**. "Dry Chemical" = Class A/B/C; "Dry Powder" = Class D |
| Flammable liquids | Within code limits; use approved flammable-storage refrigerators |
| Obstructions | Keep strobes, alarms, panels, sprinklers, exits clear |
| PPE | Minimum lab attire at all times: **full-length pants and closed-toe, closed-heel shoes**; more per hazard assessment/HCP |
| Sprinklers | **Minimum 18 inches** clearance below sprinkler heads; no pyrophorics in unsprinklered buildings |

Lesson: in one campus lab fire, an obstruction kept the nearest sprinkler from activating; the fire spread, three sprinklers activated, and water flooded floors down to the basement.
