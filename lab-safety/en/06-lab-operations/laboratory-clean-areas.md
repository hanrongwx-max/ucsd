# Laboratory Clean Areas

"Designate a laboratory Clean Area where eating and drinking are permitted."

## Requirement
Eating, drinking, food storage, applying cosmetics, and handling contact lenses are allowed in research laboratories **only in approved, posted Clean Areas**. Clean Areas may not be in rooms where aerosol transmissible pathogens are manipulated, or in BSL-2+ or BSL-3 rooms.

## Designating a Clean Area
Must be: **separated** from lab work areas by at least **one meter** or a substantial physical barrier (solid bookcase, acrylic shield); **approved** by EH&S; **posted** with a Clean Area sign; **equipped** with a non-laboratory trash receptacle.

## Lab Freezers and Refrigerators
No food in lab refrigerators/freezers; post a warning sign; assign someone to check periodically.

## Protecting Clean Areas
Remove gloves and **wash hands** before entering — hand sanitizer is not a substitute. Survey Clean Area boundaries for radioactive contamination where applicable.
