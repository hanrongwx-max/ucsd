# Chemical Fume Hoods Overview

All chemical fume hoods must meet Title 8, CCR §5154.1.

## Purpose
Among the most reliable lab engineering controls when used properly: they contain and remove contaminants via the lab exhaust, support room ventilation, and the sash shields the worker from splashes, fires, or minor explosions.

## Types
- **Constant Air Volume (CAV):** same exhaust at any sash position; face velocity changes with the sash. EH&S marks the correct sash opening.
- **Variable Air Volume (VAV):** maintains **100 fpm** face velocity at all sash heights; monitor shows standard/standby mode; emergency purge button; audible alarm on malfunction — call Facilities Management (858) 534-2930.

## Limitations
- **Not for biohazardous materials** — use a biological safety cabinet.
- Highly toxic materials may require a glove box (ask ehschem@ucsd.edu).
- **Ductless fume hoods are not allowed at UC San Diego.**

## Certification
EH&S inspects and tests every hood **annually**; a sticker shows results. Tests: room negative pressure; sash movement; audio and visual alarms; face velocity **100 lfm**; overall condition. Suspected problems: ehsweb@ucsd.edu, (858) 534-5427.
