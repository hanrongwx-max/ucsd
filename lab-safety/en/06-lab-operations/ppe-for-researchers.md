# PPE for Researchers

UC San Diego's department-funded laboratory PPE Distribution Program.

## Program
Each eligible lab member receives **two lab coats and one pair of safety glasses** for their time at UC San Diego. Face shields and splash goggles available by special request. Fitting: in person (PPE Office booking) or virtual (online form). You must first be on your PI's LHAT personnel list.

PPE Office: Hopkins Parking Structure, 2nd floor; Mon/Wed/Fri 9:00–3:00; ehs-ppe@ucsd.edu, (858) 534-2698.

## Eligibility
- **Personal coat:** paid undergraduates, graduate students, postdocs, staff working in the lab at least one year.
- **Loaner coat:** volunteers and researchers in the lab less than one year (return on leaving).
- Affiliates without SSO: register an affiliate account via the DSA, then email ehsrap@ucsd.edu to be added to the EH&S database.
- **Every lab member must be personally fitted for a lab coat and safety glasses.**

## Responsibilities
All faculty, students, staff, and visitors must wear proper PPE in labs. **PIs are responsible for selecting and requiring PPE** and should complete the LHAT. Additional PPE (gloves, face shields, goggles/sleeves) is requested by the PI or ASC.

Prescription safety eyewear is paid by the department (ordered through Oracle / Fisher / Hoya).

## Lab Coat Laundry (free)
Pre-labeled envelope from Lab Manager/ASC → label name, PI, building, room → empty pockets, fold a **dry, decontaminated** coat, one per envelope → campus mail or laundry mailbox (pickups Wednesdays). Returns within two weeks.
- If a coat may be contaminated with hazardous material, do **not** send it — contact ehs-ppe@ucsd.edu.
- Coats must be laundered by a compliant service (CCR Title 8 §5193). **Do not take coats home.**

## FAQ highlights
- Shared general-use lab coats are **not** acceptable — Cal/OSHA Title 8 §3380 requires a personally fitted coat and safety glasses.
- UV-blocking (99.9%) glasses are available.
- One replacement pair of safety glasses is provided; after that the lab buys them.
