# Report a Work-Related Injury, Illness, or Hazardous Material Exposure

## Important Notices
- "Campus-funded employees must notify their supervisor and complete an incident report." UC San Diego Health-funded employees call (619) 543-3200.
- Report all work-related deaths, catastrophes, and serious injuries or illnesses to Workers' Compensation at **(858) 534-0076**; after hours UC San Diego Police **(858) 534-4357**.
- The Workers' Compensation Office must report serious injuries to Cal/OSHA **within 8 hours**; delays can result in fines to the department.
- **Serious** = requires inpatient hospitalization, loss of any body member, or serious permanent disfigurement.

## Steps
1. **Notify your supervisor immediately.**
2. **Report the incident:** online Employee's and Supervisor's Occupational Incident Reports, or call 877-6UC-RPRT (877-682-7778) and the supervisor completes the online Supervisor Report. Students and volunteers use the Student/Volunteer Incident Report. An incident report does **not** file a workers' compensation claim.
3. **Get medical treatment:** UC San Diego Occupational Health clinic (weekdays 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.) — La Jolla, Triton Center, 9520 Gilman Dr., Suite 3014 B, (858) 657-1600; Hillcrest, 330 Lewis St., Suite 100, (619) 471-9210. Emergencies/after hours: UC San Diego Health Emergency Department (La Jolla or Hillcrest).
4. **File a workers' compensation claim** if treatment goes beyond first aid: complete and sign the DWC-1 form, describe all affected body parts, copy it, fax (858) 246-0973, mail original to Mail Code 0925.
