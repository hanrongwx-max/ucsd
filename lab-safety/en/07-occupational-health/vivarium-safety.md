# Vivarium Safety

"Find vivarium safety information for work with research animals." Vivarium and animal research follow university policy, national guidelines, industry standards, and federal, state, and county regulations.

- **Institutional Animal Care and Use Program:** Animal Care Program; IACUC and FAQ; PPM 100-6 (animal use in research and teaching).
- **Occupational Health and Safety (OHSP):** requirements for individuals exposed to research animals; risk assessment; cost of services. Related: aerosol transmissible diseases, immunocompromised researchers, high-risk agents, medical surveillance, reproductive hazards.
- **Emergency:** Emergency Guide; eye wash and showers; injury/exposure reporting.
- **Hazardous waste:** animal carcass disposal pathway (PDF); biohazardous and medical waste; radioactive waste; sharps; sewer disposal.
- **Research safety links:** anesthetic gases; animal experiments with antineoplastic or investigational drugs; chemical/biological/carcinogen safety; controlled substances; radiation safety.
- **Training:** Animal Research Training; Safety Training for Researchers.
